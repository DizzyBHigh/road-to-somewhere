DuhBuh
Ban Widget

Turn moderation into an event. A polished ban animation and timeout system designed to make moderation feel like part of your stream.

WHAT IT DOES

Built as a complete extension.

Event-level ban queueRapid bans wait their turn without starting their animation, sounds, or timers early.
Independent timeoutsTimeout cells use their own queue and remain independent from ban events.
Configurable visualsPositioning, sizing, messages, stacking, and platform-aware styling can be configured.
OBS-ready overlayUse the hosted overlay or download a local copy for your browser source.
PACKAGE

Everything you need.

The release will include the Streamer.bot import code, the required UI component, the hosted overlay, a local overlay ZIP, and step-by-step documentation.
