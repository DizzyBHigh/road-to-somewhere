# DBH Brand Guide v1

## Identity
**DuhBuhHuh / DBH**

**On the Road to Somewhere**

The road is the brand. Games are destinations, not the identity.

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Primary use |
|---|---|---|
| DBH Blue | `#0384CB` | Signature brand colour |
| Road Gold | `#F4B942` | Highlights, route markings, destination accents |
| Sunset Orange | `#F0783C` | Secondary energy accent |
| Cream | `#FFF3D6` | Main light text |
| Asphalt | `#171820` | Dark background |
| Midnight | `#0B1720` | Night mode background |
| Road Grey | `#59636A` | Secondary neutral |
| Map White | `#F2F0E8` | Light/map backgrounds |

## Palette modes

### Daylight
DBH Blue + Road Gold + Sunset Orange + Cream + Asphalt

### After Dark
DBH Blue + muted gold + Sunset Orange + Cream + Midnight

## Free fonts

- **Anton** — display/headings
- **Space Mono** — route/navigation/technical text
- **Bad Script** — small handwritten accents

The primary DuhBuhHuh logo should be a custom graphic, not a font lockup.

## Design rules
1. Keep the signature blue recognizable and intentional.
2. Use road/route language rather than game-specific branding.
3. Keep significant elements separate so OBS Move can animate them.
4. Keep live content such as game capture outside decorative PNGs.
5. Avoid paid fonts, subscriptions, or paid stock assets.
